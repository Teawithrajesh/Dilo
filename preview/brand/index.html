Rules

  • Clear space around the logo equals the height of one person-dot.
  • Minimum size: symbol 24 px, horizontal logo 96 px wide.
  • Never stretch, recolour outside the palette, add shadows, or place on busy photos without a solid backing.
  • White text never goes on orange or bright pink (too little contrast). Use navy on orange, white on pink-deep.
