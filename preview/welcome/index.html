FAQ

Questions, answered

Is DILO free?

Yes. This preview is free, and there are no ads.

Does DILO collect or hold money?

No. You give money the way you already do (cash, UPI, bank). You then record it in DILO so everyone can see the progress.

Who can see my community?

Only approved members. Communities are private by default, and new people must be approved by an admin or moderator.

Can I give anonymously?

Yes. Other members see “Anonymous”. Only the event organiser sees your name, so they can say thank you.

Do I need to install an app?

No. DILO works in your phone's browser, and you can add it to your home screen like an app.
